Privacy policy

Tasty Treats Tycoon and this page. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

In short

  • You do not need an account to play, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details in the game.
  • Your game progress is stored on your own device (and, on Steam, in Steam Cloud if you have it enabled).
  • The free Android and iOS apps offer optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. They are requested as child-directed, non-personalised and suitable for general audiences.
  • The free Android and iOS apps also offer optional one-time and repeatable in-app purchases, handled by Apple and Google, with a parental gate before every purchase.
  • The paid editions (Steam, Amazon, Samsung) have no ads and no purchases.
  • This page sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party resources.

2. The game

2.1 What the game does not collect

Tasty Treats Tycoon has no sign-in, no chat, no social features, no player profile and no analytics or tracking software of our own. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and we do not run a server that receives your game data.

2.2 Your saved game

Your progress (coins, unlocked islands, settings and similar game data) is stored locally. It contains no information that identifies you.

EditionWhere the save is kept
Android, iOSOn your device, in the app’s own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you have device backups turned on (Google or iCloud), the save may be included in your device’s own backup.
Steam (Windows, macOS, Linux)As a file in the app’s data folder on your computer, and synced by Valve through Steam Cloud if you have it enabled. Valve is responsible for Steam Cloud and Steam achievements under its own privacy policy.
Amazon, Samsung (paid Android edition)On your device, in the app’s own storage. If you have device backups turned on (Google), the save may be included in your device’s own backup. This edition contains no ad or billing code.

2.3 Ads (Android and iOS, free editions)

The free mobile apps can show optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, and Google LLC elsewhere). Because the game is made for players of all ages, including children, we configure the ad requests as follows:

  • requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent;
  • ads are non-personalised: they are not based on your interests or past behaviour;
  • only ads rated for general audiences are requested;
  • on Android we have removed the permission to read the Google advertising ID from the app.

To deliver an ad, measure whether it was shown and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes technical information on your device. According to Google this can include your IP address and the approximate (country or region-level) location derived from it, app and ad interactions, crash logs and diagnostics, and device or app-scoped identifiers. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob’s treatment of child-directed apps. Our legal basis for offering rewarded ads is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in financing a free game); where the SDK stores or reads information on your device, it does so only as technically necessary for delivering the ad and for security and fraud prevention.

The app does not show its own advertising consent dialog, because it requests only non-personalised, child-directed ads. You can skip ads completely: they are always optional, and the Sugar Pass makes the ad bonuses available without watching an ad.

2.4 Purchases (Android and iOS, free editions)

The free mobile apps offer optional in-app purchases (coin packs, boost boxes, a Starter Pack, the Sugar Pass and a Dress-Up Pack). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see or store your payment details; the store only tells the game which item was bought, so that it can be delivered. We do not operate our own purchase server and do not keep purchase records linked to you.

Parental gate: before any purchase or restore, the game asks a simple sum that a grown-up can solve in seconds. Nothing is remembered. On children’s devices, parents can also use Apple’s “Ask to Buy” or Google Play’s parental controls and Family Link.

2.5 Steam, Amazon and Samsung editions

These editions contain no ads and no in-app purchases. The Steam edition uses Steamworks features (Steam Cloud and achievements), which are operated by Valve; Valve’s privacy policy applies to your Steam account. The Amazon and Samsung editions are bought through Amazon or Samsung respectively, who process your purchase under their own terms.

3. Children

Tasty Treats Tycoon is made for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, or from anyone, in the game. We configure ads as child-directed and non-personalised, protect purchases with a parental gate, and offer no chat or social features, no external links in the game and no random-reward boxes. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information about your child has reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. Because we hold no personal data about players in the game, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you are always welcome to ask: write to support@unikkorn.xyz.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen (LfD), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, lfd.niedersachsen.de. You may also contact the authority in your own country.
